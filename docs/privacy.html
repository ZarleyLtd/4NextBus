4NextBus privacy policy

4NextBus is an Alexa skill published by Zarley Ltd. It tells you the next buses, Luases, DART trains, or local commuter trains using publicly available data from the National Transport Authority.

What we store

If you set a favourite, we store that stop or station (and direction, where needed) against the Amazon account identifier Alexa sends us. You can have one favourite for buses, one for LUAS, one for Dart, and one for local train. We do not receive your name, email address, or home address. We do not use the device location.

What we do not do

The skill does not sell data, show advertising, or share a favourite with anyone else. Bus, LUAS, DART, and local train times are fetched from the National Transport Authority when you ask, and are not stored as a personal travel history.

How to remove your favourite

Say "Alexa, ask four next bus to forget my favourite stop" or "unset my favourite stop" to delete your bus favourite. For another mode, say "forget my favourite LUAS stop", "forget my favourite Dart stop", or "forget my favourite local train stop". Unset works in place of forget. To delete every saved favourite, say "forget all my favourite stops".
